# Blood Connection design system

An initiative by Bindubashini Boys’ 2015 & Friends.

Use this single signature without an expanded “Created by” line. The blood-drop emblem contains the exact white handshake illustration selected by the user, including its wrist details. Keep the same hand anatomy across all surfaces; do not substitute a hand-drawn approximation. `brand/handshake-drop.png` is the transparent artwork master, and `mark.svg` is a generated layout wrapper referencing that raster artwork; the admin uses a rendered PNG. Regenerate every platform icon and the mobile asset with `python tools/generate_icons.py`. Flutter uses the same warm-white square and 202/1024 padding as the wrapper. The source image's own transparent margins keep the visible shape inside Android's 66×66dp safe area. Android uses separate background and full-colour foreground layers; a monochrome layer is omitted because flattening the entire raster alpha would erase the white handshake. No trademark clearance is claimed. See `brand/README.md` for the image-generation prompt and provenance.

Flutter Material 3, native controls, warm white canvas (#FAF8F5), deep ink (#203135), crimson primary (#B42346), calm teal secondary (#176B65), quiet mint surfaces (#E6F3EF), rose surfaces (#F8E8EC), muted text (#59686B). These tokens also apply to browser administration. No decorative gradients, low-contrast neumorphism, fake testimonials, or donor-map pins revealing precise locations. UI/UX Pro Max healthcare results are relevant to trust and legibility; their landing-page/testimonial and neumorphic recommendations do not fit urgent donor search. Use a clear map, explicit filters and accessible cards instead.

Bundle Manrope locally under its SIL Open Font License: 400 body, 600 labels, 700 headings, with system fallback for unsupported scripts. No font-service requests. Use 16px body text where space allows, restrained negative heading tracking, 16–24px gutters, 12–24px corners and at least 48dp controls. Five destinations: Home, Find donors, Requests, Messages, Profile. Native text scaling and reduced-motion settings remain respected. Wide screens use a navigation rail. Local builds retain a visible demo label; all result flows provide empty, loading, error and retry states.

Home prioritizes Find a donor and donation availability. Search fills its content area with a freely pannable map, floating blood-group/range controls at the safe top edge, and a native draggable donor sheet at the bottom. Horizontal filter scrolling preserves room for the map. Group/count bubbles represent stable approximate areas, never donor addresses; tapping one expands its matching donor cards. Explicit Expand, Collapse, All areas, zoom and recenter controls make gestures optional. Sheet scrolling and map panning have separate touch regions. Moving the map does not change the saved search origin or silently issue new searches. The accepted meeting destination remains separate from these discovery areas.

Profile holds private measurements, BMI, automatic/manual location and donation log. Height accepts centimetres, metres, inches or feet/inches; weight accepts kilograms, pounds or stone/pounds. Remember display units per account on the device, keep canonical centimetres/kilograms for saving and BMI, and stack paired inputs at large text sizes. Native location determines country and area together; manual entry asks for a place, not coordinates. Never imply BMI or the cooldown establishes clinical eligibility. Donor results show alias, group, city, coarse distance and a coarse map area. Photos remain restricted to accepted matches. Authentication uses only Google, Apple and Facebook, plus explicitly marked development entry. Members use mobile only; administrators have mobile and browser workspaces.

Motion communicates selection and navigation: a brief marker lift, native blood-chip feedback, map inertia and a short donor-sheet transition. Explicit zoom and recenter use a 280ms camera transition, interrupted immediately by touch or wheel input. Initial framing is immediate, and a delayed search cannot undo a user's map pan. Respect the system's reduced-motion preference by applying camera/selection/sheet changes immediately. Show actual search progress in the pinned header and announce results with semantics. Avoid continuous pulses, artificial live activity or decorative animation that obscures matching. Profile options use visible headings, units and plain-language sharing/setup explanations; destructive actions retain explicit confirmation.

Shared timing uses 120ms press/marker feedback, 200ms selection/result feedback and 280ms spatial travel. Welcome and Home have a finite 10dp entrance movement, with content interactive immediately and opacity starting at 0.96 to retain readable text contrast. Covered routes and reduced-motion settings complete the reveal without queuing a replay. Replace old content immediately; do not retain a previous account, donor result or private image for a crossfade. Search keeps a fixed progress slot and a stable map element while filters change; reduced-motion loading uses a static icon and visible text. Navigation uses outlined idle icons and filled selected icons from the bundled Material family, with at least 48dp icon-button targets. The Home connection motif is decorative and static; the approved handshake logo remains unchanged.

Home uses a compact donation-status row with an icon, Available to donate/Offline/In cooldown text, a direct availability switch outside recovery and a visible countdown during recovery. The full policy explanation, after-cooldown preference, record action and history live in an accessible Donation settings bottom sheet; Profile retains the detailed status. Keep targets at least 48dp and allow text to grow rather than clipping. Either matched participant can confirm a completed donation; use donor/seeker wording and make clear whose history and cooldown will change. No donation dismisses only the caller's reminder. Both parties can report from requests or chat, with visible evidence upload progress and honest partial-save feedback. Reports and attachments stay private to the reporter and authorized moderation view; never render user filenames or evidence as HTML.

My reports is reachable from Profile and Requests. Show pending/reviewed status, submission/review time when known, a plain-language outcome and private evidence. Keep internal moderation notes private. Review notifications open the owner's report in its original country without changing the member's current location. Report review updates remain available while the donor is offline or in recovery; donor-request eligibility rules are separate.
